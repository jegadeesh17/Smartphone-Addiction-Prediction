# Product Brief: Smartphone Addiction Analytical Platform

## Vision & Objective
An authoritative, responsive web application for smartphone addiction risk assessment and population cohort analytics. It bridges competitive tabular machine learning with accessible digital well-being diagnostics, delivering sub-20ms live predictions alongside demographic benchmark insights.

---

## Core Capabilities & Feature Hierarchy

### 1. Primary Diagnostic Engine (Live Assessment)
- Dynamic behavioral profile adjustments via sliders and segmented pills.
- Instant LightGBM Fold 1 inference with exact population priors.
- Real-time risk probability gauge and categorical classification badge (`ADDICTION DETECTED` vs `HEALTHY`).
- Granular diagnostic ratios (`Screen / Sleep`, `Recreational Share`, `Avg Session Length`, `Weekend Surge`).
- Rule-based behavioral hygiene recommendations.

### 2. Population Cohort Analytics Explorer
- Interactive demographic slicing across 691,369 participant records.
- Distribution overlays for screen time, sleep duration, and interaction density.
- Population quantile benchmarks (25th, 50th, 75th, 90th percentiles) plotted against the user's personal inputs.

### 3. What-If Scenario Simulator & Habit Planner
- Interactive habit counterfactual sliders (e.g. reduce social media by 1.5h, sleep 1h more).
- Real-time probability delta display (`-18.4% Risk Reduction`).
- Factor attribution identifying the most influential drivers of individual risk.

---

## Target Audience & Tonality
- **Audience**: Digital well-being coaches, clinical researchers, and self-assessors.
- **Tone**: Authoritative, analytical, calm, and objective. Avoids gamification, alarmist sensationalism, and toy aesthetics.
